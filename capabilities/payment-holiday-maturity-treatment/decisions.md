## Question resolution and remaining decisions

Q-001 core scope is resolved by D-002. Q-002 interest treatment is resolved by D-003; D-006 resolves spreading across remaining repayments. D-017 confirms existing applicable allocation rules for voluntary holiday payments. Mapping separately accrued holiday interest into those rules and policy configuration remain to establish. Q-003 initial exit treatment is resolved by D-004; D-006 confirms equal total repayments with a final rounding adjustment; detailed calculation remains open. Earlier recommendations below are historical proposals where they conflict with these decisions; confirmed decisions govern.

Q-007 post-start change/cancellation scope is resolved by D-013: deferred from the initial release. Repeat requests and unsupported-operation responses remain open.

Q-004 version-creation choice is resolved by D-007. D-011 resolves effective date and operative-version selection: the new version operates from holiday commencement, with the existing version operative before then. D-012 resolves pre-start cancellation: retain the cancelled future version and keep the existing schedule operative. Exact day-boundary handling remains open. Preserve the previous version without rewriting historical obligations.

Q-005 operational DPD policy is resolved by D-008. Holiday days do not increase operational DPD; no second total-days measure is requested. D-009 resolves pausing late-payment penalties during the holiday. D-010 preserves existing penalty balances and permits post-holiday penalties only for remaining overdue amounts, without holiday catch-up. D-014 allows accounts with existing arrears and keeps those amounts separately outstanding, outside automatic holiday repayment spreading. Collection handling, allocation and other eligibility rules remain open. Historical evidence of ordinary ageing is not the selected holiday target policy.
