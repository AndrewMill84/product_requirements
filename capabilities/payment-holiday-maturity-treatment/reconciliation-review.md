## Confirmed policy is settled

D-001–D-023 remain in force. The initial treatment is a fixed-rate monthly annuity holiday covering one to six instalments, with a later repayment before unchanged maturity. Interest accrues separately and never earns interest. Post-holiday total repayments are equal apart from final rounding. Approval creates a future-effective contractual version; original history remains. DPD and late penalties pause over the agreed inclusive holiday days. Existing arrears/penalties remain separate. Voluntary receipts use existing allocation rules and do not automatically rewrite the contract. Preview refresh is mandatory after relevant pre-approval change.

Term extension, capitalisation and a final residual-payment strategy are not open initial-release choices. Post-start holiday changes/cancellations are deferred. Technical gaps do not reopen those decisions.

## Recommended numerical method — not approved policy

R-001 needs a worked fixture before readiness. For an account with no old arrears, let P be eligible principal, H separately accrued/projected holiday interest and n remaining repayment dates.

Proposed components: h_j = rounded H/n for the first n−1 rows; the last h_j is the unpaid remainder. For each period, simulate ordinary interest I_j from its calendar days using existing daily interest posting, internal precision and currency rounding. Choose the level principal-plus-ordinary-interest amount A by solving the resulting amortisation path; total regular repayment is A + the regular h_j. Principal repayment is A − I_j. Adjust the final row only to clear the remaining principal and holiday-interest remainder within unchanged maturity.

Do not use an ordinary annuity formula on P+H: that would charge interest on holiday interest. Do not substitute whole-period rounding for daily postings. No recommended h_j rule is confirmed yet. Accounts with old arrears and changing actuals additionally require R-002/R-003. Final numerical fixtures must reconcile principal, ordinary interest, holiday interest, cash and ledger entries independently.
