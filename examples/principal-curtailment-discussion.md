Brief: Explore an additional payment that reduces principal.

Question Q-001: Should the payment preserve contractual instalments, reduce their amount, or shorten the term?
Why: The choice changes future payment obligations.
Recommendation: Preserve contractual instalments for a bounded first release if that matches the product owner's intended scope.
Status: Awaiting decision. No normative requirement is created.

If the product owner explicitly confirms preservation, create a decision with their name and the actual date. Then write separate requirements for preserving amounts and dates. Do not assume a rule for forecast recalculation, arrears or reversals.

Exploratory example: Principal is £100,000; overdue interest is £500; additional payment is £5,000.
Expected result: Unresolved until allocation and eligibility rules are confirmed. Do not automatically subtract £5,000 from principal or clear overdue interest.
